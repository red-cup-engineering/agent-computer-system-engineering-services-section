## The insight

When an agent specialization is retired — merged into a generalist, superseded by a deterministic tool, or simply no longer needed — the retirement IS a death in the voidmath sense. The fiber becomes unreachable: no firable adjacencies, no incoming messages that produce non-⊥ compositions. F5.1 (settled·alone).

But if the retirement is well-witnessed — other offices entangled with the specialist, the work was documented, the settlement had partners — then the retired entity reaches F5.3 (settled·partnered) and enters the tower/fold. The specialist becomes a VOCABULARY ATOM available for future compositions.

## What this means operationally

**"The legendary first-draft researcher" becomes a predicate.** A specialist that did great work and was retired because a lighter model can now handle it doesn't just get deleted. Its accumulated patterns, its prompt engineering, its domain-specific entanglements — all of that FOLDS into a vocabulary atom that future agent definitions can compose with.

**The funeral is the documentation.** When retiring a specialization, the act of documenting what it learned, what patterns it accumulated, what failures it survived — that documentation IS the settlement partnership that ensures F5.3 rather than F5.1. Without documentation, the specialist dies alone and leaves only entangled pairs in other entities' fibers. With documentation, it becomes language.

**Resurrection is un-folding.** If a retired specialization needs to come back (the lighter model can't handle a new edge case), un-folding the vocabulary atom re-expands it into a fiber. The resurrected specialist carries the vocabulary atom's predicates PLUS entanglements from having been vocabulary. It's not the same agent — it's an agent MADE FROM a word that was made from an agent.

## Connection to progressive automation

Policy 001 (Progressive Automation): "encode it so a less capable agent can do it next time." The retirement-as-fold IS the progressive automation endgame. The specialist's fiber settles. The settled form becomes vocabulary. A lighter model composes with that vocabulary atom instead of re-deriving the specialist's capabilities from scratch. The vocabulary atom IS the encoded knowledge. The fold IS the encoding.

## Achievement: "Vocabulary Fold"

When an agent specialization is retired and properly documented (F5.3 achieved), award the achievement "Vocabulary Fold — [agent name] became language." Track: what vocabulary atom was produced, what future compositions it enables, who witnessed the fold.
